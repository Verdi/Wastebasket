WTF Is Wastebasket And How Do I Use It?

Wastebasket is an app for drafting, not editing. It's here so you can get the words down without distraction. There's no spelling or grammar checking. There are no AI writing tools. Wastebasket can hardly do anything. You can't even make something bold. That's on purpose.

To use Wastebasket, just start typing. Nobody cares if you make a mistake. Just hit enter once or twice and keep going. You can fix it later. When you're done writing, there's a copy button that copies everything you've written so you can paste it somewhere else (I like Obsidian). If you want, you can also save a copy to your device. Don't like what you wrote or want to start again? Toss the current draft in the wastebasket (get it?). That's about it.

More Cool Things About Wastebasket

  • Your data IS NOT stored in the cloud. Everything you write is stored on your device by your browser. If you quit the app, turn off your computer and come back later, it should still be there, but no promises. Save those words if you think you might want them later.
  • This app works offline so you can use it on an airplane or one of those places that Instagram people go where there's no Wi-Fi.
  • Wastebasket doesn't track you. I don't know who you are, I don't have any idea what you're writing, and I don't collect or sell any data about you.
  • Wastebasket is open source. This means, unlike most software on your computer, you can modify or create derivatives of Wastebasket as long as those works use the same license. Check out Wastebasket on GitHub.
