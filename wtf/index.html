WTF Is This App?

Wastebasket is an app for drafting, not editing. It's here so you can get the words down without distraction. There's no spelling or grammar checking. There are no AI writing tools. Wastebasket can hardly do anything. You can't even copy and paste. That's on purpose.

To use Wastebasket, just start typing. Nobody cares if you make a mistake. Just hit enter once or twice and keep going. You can fix it later. When you're done writing, there's a copy button that copies everything you've written so you can paste it somewhere else (I like Obsidian). If you want, you can also save a plain text file to your device. Don't like what you wrote or want to start again? Toss the current draft in the wastebasket. That's about it.

By the way, this website doesn't store anything in the clould. Everything you write is stored on your device by your browser. If you quit the app, turn off your computer and come back later it should still be there but no promises. Save those words if you think you might want them later.

This app also works offline so you can use it on an airplane or one of those places that Instagram people go where there's no wifi.

There are analytics (they from XXX not Google) that I collect but I'm not tracking you, I don't know who you are and I don't have any idea what you're writing. I do this so I can see how many people are using the app and whether there seems to be any problems. This app also doesn't use any remotely hosted fonts or other resources.
